# Distributed Monolithic

**What it is:** A monolith — one codebase/deployable — that is scaled out horizontally behind a load balancer, often backed by a shared database.

**What it's used for:** Balancing the simplicity of a monolith with the capacity of multiple instances. A pragmatic middle ground before splitting into microservices.
