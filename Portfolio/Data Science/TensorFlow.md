# TensorFlow

**What it is:** Google's open-source deep-learning framework with static-graph execution, Keras high-level API, and large-scale production tooling (TF Serving, Lite, JS).

**What it's used for:** Building, training, and deploying neural networks at scale — mobile, web, on-prem, and cloud inference.
